**Problem 1 (Thermodynamics and the First Law):** ΔG° = −20 kJ/mol at 298 K. Is K > 1? Estimate magnitude.

**Answer:**

Yes. K = exp(−ΔG°/RT) = exp(20000/(8.314×298)) ≈ **exp(8.07) ≈ 3200**. Real life: binding constants for drug–receptor follow same relation.
