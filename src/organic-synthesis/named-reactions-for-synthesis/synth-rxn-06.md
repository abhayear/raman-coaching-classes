## Worked example with answer

**Example (try first):**

Convert benzene → nitrobenzene → aniline in 2 steps.

**Real-life link:** Detergent alkylbenzenes (LAB) are made by Friedel-Crafts alkylation at petrochemical plants — the sulfonate in your laundry liquid starts here.

---

**Answer:**

(1) HNO₃/H₂SO₄ → nitrobenzene (EAS). (2) Sn/HCl or H₂/Pd → **aniline**. Real life: aniline is dye and polymer precursor.
