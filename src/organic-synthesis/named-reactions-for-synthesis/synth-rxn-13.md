## Worked example with answer

**Example (try first):**

PCC oxidises primary alcohol to ___? Jones reagent?

**Real-life link:** Apple slices brown via enzymatic oxidation (polyphenol oxidase); Swern and PCC in the lab selectively oxidize alcohols to carbonyls for drug intermediates.

---

**Answer:**

PCC → **aldehyde** (stops at aldehyde). Jones (CrO₃/H₂SO₄) → **carboxylic acid**. Real life: selective oxidation prevents over-oxidation in fragrance aldehyde synthesis.
