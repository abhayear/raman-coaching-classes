Try each problem first, then check the answer shown directly below.

**Problem 1 (Acetals):** Convert benzene → nitrobenzene → aniline in 2 steps.

**Answer:**

(1) HNO₃/H₂SO₄ → nitrobenzene (EAS). (2) Sn/HCl or H₂/Pd → **aniline**. Real life: aniline is dye and polymer precursor.
