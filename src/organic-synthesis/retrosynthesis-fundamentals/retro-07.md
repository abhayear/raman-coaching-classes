## Worked example with answer

**Example (try first):**

Retrosynthesize **butan-2-ol** in ≤2 steps from ≤3 C building blocks.

**Real-life link:** Police breathalysers and greenhouse gas monitors use IR absorption; your TV remote sends IR pulses — bond vibrations absorb specific wavelengths.

---

**Answer:**

Disconnection: C–OH bond → **Grignard**. Forward: (1) Mg/CH₃CH₂Br → CH₃CH₂MgBr; (2) + CH₃CHO; (3) H₃O⁺ → butan-2-ol. Real life: same logic used to plan paracetamol from p-aminophenol + acetic anhydride.

(1) HNO₃/H₂SO₄ → nitrobenzene (EAS). (2) Sn/HCl or H₂/Pd → **aniline**. Real life: aniline is dye and polymer precursor.
