## Core concepts (each linked to real life)

### 1. Synthons

In Organic Synthesis, Synthons is best understood as a pattern you recognize, not a
definition to memorize. Start from everyday analogies and build upward.

**Real-life connection:** Planning a multi-step drug route is like planning a road trip with toll booths:
disconnect the target into milestones you can buy commercially — chemists call those
synthons.

**Why it matters:** MSc-level work assumes you can predict behavior involving synthons and justify your
reasoning with evidence (spectra, rates, stereochemistry, or thermodynamics).
