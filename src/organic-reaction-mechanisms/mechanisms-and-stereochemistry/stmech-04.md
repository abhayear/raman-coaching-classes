**Real-life link:** Enzymes in yeast ferment bread and beer; Pt in car exhaust neutralizes pollutants — catalysts lower activation energy everywhere from kitchen to highway.

**Answer:**

**Problem 1 (Surface catalysts):** Primary vs tertiary alkyl halide + NaI in acetone — which reacts faster?

**Answer:**

**Primary/methyl faster** (SN2). Tertiary blocked sterically; may go SN1 if polar protic solvent used.

---

**Problem 2 (Surface catalysts):** Is CH₃OH a good nucleophile in acid or base?

**Answer:**

In **base**: CH₃O⁻ is strong nucleophile. In **acid**: mostly CH₃OH (neutral), poor nucleophile; protonation of alcohol gives R–OH₂⁺ (leaving group in substitution).
