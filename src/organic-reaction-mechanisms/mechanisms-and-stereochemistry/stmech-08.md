## Worked example with answer

**Example (try first):**

Is CH₃OH a good nucleophile in acid or base?

**Real-life link:** Mirror-image limonene smells like orange (R) vs pine (S) — stereochemistry is not abstract; it's why enantiopure drugs cost more to manufacture.

---

**Answer:**

In **base**: CH₃O⁻ is strong nucleophile. In **acid**: mostly CH₃OH (neutral), poor nucleophile; protonation of alcohol gives R–OH₂⁺ (leaving group in substitution). **Advanced extension:** Propose one experiment (IR, rate study, isotope label) to confirm this answer.

**Problem 1 (Racemization coupled to selective reaction):** Primary vs tertiary alkyl halide + NaI in acetone — which reacts faster?

**Answer:**

**Primary/methyl faster** (SN2). Tertiary blocked sterically; may go SN1 if polar protic solvent used.
