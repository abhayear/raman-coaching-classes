## Worked example with answer

**Example (try first):**

(CH₃)₃CBr in ethanol/water gives two products. Identify them and the rate law.

**Real-life link:** Mirror-image limonene smells like orange (R) vs pine (S) — stereochemistry is not abstract; it's why enantiopure drugs cost more to manufacture.

---

**Answer:**

Rate = k[(CH₃)₃CBr] only (unimolecular). Products: **(CH₃)₃COH** (SN1, solvolysis) and **(CH₃)₂C=CH₂** (E1, minor at low T). Carbocation (CH₃)₃C⁺ intermediate; racemization if chiral centre at C–Br.
