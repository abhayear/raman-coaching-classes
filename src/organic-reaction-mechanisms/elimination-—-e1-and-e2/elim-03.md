## Worked example with answer

**Example (try first):**

2-bromobutane + t-BuOK in t-BuOH at high T. Major alkene?

**Real-life link:** Octane rating in petrol relates to smooth combustion; branched alkanes resist forming unstable primary carbocations during engine knocking — stability rules mat…

---

**Answer:**

**E2**, anti-periplanar H and Br. With bulky base t-BuOK → **Hofmann product 1-butene** (less substituted) often competes; from 2-bromobutane major is **trans-2-butene** (Zaitsev, more stable).

## Practice problems with answers

Try each problem first, then check the answer shown directly below.

**Problem 1 (Carbocation):** Is CH₃OH a good nucleophile in acid or base?

**Answer:**

In **base**: CH₃O⁻ is strong nucleophile. In **acid**: mostly CH₃OH (neutral), poor nucleophile; protonation of alcohol gives R–OH₂⁺ (leaving group in substitution).

---

**Problem 2 (Conjugate base intermediate):** Primary vs tertiary alkyl halide + NaI in acetone — which reacts faster?

**Answer:**

**Primary/methyl faster** (SN2). Tertiary blocked sterically; may go SN1 if polar protic solvent used.
