**Problem 1 (Resonance stabilization):** (R)-2-bromobutane is treated with NaCN in acetone. Predict the stereochemistry of the product and name the mechanism.

**Answer:**

SN2 — backside attack inverts configuration. (R)-2-bromobutane → **(S)-2-methylbutanenitrile**. Rate = k[substrate][CN⁻]; polar aprotic acetone stabilizes CN⁻ without strong solvation shell.

---

**Problem 2 (Competition with SN1):** Compare reaction rate: CH₃Br vs (CH₃)₃CBr with I⁻ in acetone.

**Answer:**

CH₃Br reacts **much faster** (SN2). Methyl/primary halides have minimal steric hindrance; tert-butyl blocks backside approach. (CH₃)₃CBr favours SN1/E1, not SN2.
