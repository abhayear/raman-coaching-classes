## Practice problems with answers

Try each problem first, then check the answer shown directly below.

**Problem 1 (Hard-soft):** Primary vs tertiary alkyl halide + NaI in acetone — which reacts faster?

**Answer:**

**Primary/methyl faster** (SN2). Tertiary blocked sterically; may go SN1 if polar protic solvent used.

---

**Problem 2 (Thermodynamic vs kinetic enolates):** ΔG° = −20 kJ/mol at 298 K. Is K > 1? Estimate magnitude.

**Answer:**

Yes. K = exp(−ΔG°/RT) = exp(20000/(8.314×298)) ≈ **exp(8.07) ≈ 3200**. Real life: binding constants for drug–receptor follow same relation.
